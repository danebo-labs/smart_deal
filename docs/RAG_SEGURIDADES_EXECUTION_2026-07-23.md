## Surgical citation-contract execution

The final implementation follows the native Bedrock citation contract:

- `generation.txt` contains no `<DOC_REFS>` protocol and ends with the single
  `$output_format_instructions$` output contract.
- `stop_sequences: ["</DOC_REFS>"]` and `normalize_doc_refs_tag` are removed.
- `doc_refs` is built deterministically from citation metadata, with
  `fallback_retrieve` when citations are absent.
- Visible `[n]` markers use Bedrock generated-response spans.
- The answer-safety processor runs once, inside `BedrockRagService`, with cited
  chunks or fallback chunks as its existence-validation evidence.
- A canned `Sorry` with retrieved evidence is distinguished from a genuine
  no-results outcome in diagnostics and quality telemetry.
